# The Stem-and-Beam System (For Tab-Only Layouts)

If you do not want the clutter of a musical staff, you can borrow
the "rhythm stem" system used in guitar tablature.

* **Quarter Notes:** Place a single vertical line (stem) directly below or
  above the whistle graphic.
* **Eighth Notes:** Connect two adjacent whistle graphics with a horizontal bar
  (beam) spanning across their stems.
* **Dotted Notes:** Place a small dot next to the graphic or its stem.
* **Bar Lines:** Retain standard vertical bar lines | between your whistle
  graphics to mark the musical measures according to your time signature.
